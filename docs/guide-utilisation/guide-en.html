02 · Finding your way around

Get your bearings in 30 seconds.

Every page can be reached from the menu at the top of the screen, organized into 4 sections. Click a section to open its pages.

The header

  • Logo (left): always takes you back to the home page.
  • 4 sections: The Association · Understand Stroke · News · Get Involved.
  • EN / FR: switches the language of the current page.
  • Donate (blue button): opens the donation panel.
  • Breadcrumb below the header (e.g. Home › The Association › Governance): shows where you are.

Choosing the language

The website opens in English by default. To switch to French, click FR at the top right (the button shows EN when you are already in French). The web address changes accordingly:

strokeaction.org/en/… English
strokeaction.org/fr/… French

On a phone

The menu is grouped behind the ☰ icon at the top right. Tap a section to expand it.

💡 Tip — send the right language straight away When sharing a link with a French speaker, send the /fr/ version (e.g. strokeaction.org/fr/contact): they land directly on the French page.

04 · Pages to know

The 4 pages every member should master.

These are the pages you will most often point people to when they ask you questions.

① Stroke Education

Menu › Understand Stroke › Stroke Education. The heart of our awareness mission: the FAST method, risk factors (modifiable or not), key figures and the "Did You Know?" section.

② Governance

Menu › The Association › Governance. Presents the 5 Executive Board members: role, short bio and email address. Click "Read more" to see the full biography.

Menu › The Association › Key Documents. Click a document's row to open / download the PDF. Greyed-out documents are marked "Coming soon".

05 · Get involved

Join, volunteer, donate: step by step.

Everything happens on the Support Us page (Menu › Get Involved). Each button opens a side panel explaining the procedure.

Becoming a member / volunteer

  1. Click "Apply for Membership" (or "Get Involved" to volunteer).
  2. Download the Word form (.docx), in English or French.
  3. Fill it in: full name, date of birth, email, phone, profession, city / country, motivation + copy of a photo ID.
  4. Send it back to contact@strokeaction.org or via WhatsApp at +237 652 14 81 47.
  5. The Executive Board reviews the application and sends a confirmation letter.
Good to know
  • Active membership: 18 years or older (Bylaws, Art. 7).
  • Volunteering is open to all skills, whatever your background.

06 · The contact form

Writing to the association.

The Contact Us page (Menu › Get Involved › Contact Us) brings together our contact details and a form. Every message sent goes straight to the contact@strokeaction.org inbox.

Filling in the form

  1. Complete the required fields *: full name, email, subject, message.
  2. Optional: phone number and preferred reply channel (email, phone, WhatsApp).
  3. Optional: attach one file (e.g. proof of payment) — 5 MB maximum.
  4. Click "Send Message". The "Message sent!" notice confirms it went through.

Messages can be written in English or in French.

What happens next

  • The message arrives by email with the subject [Contact] + subject.
  • Simply click "Reply": the answer goes straight to the sender.
  • Name, phone and preferred channel appear at the top of the message.
If an error message appears Check the required fields and the attachment size (≤ 5 MB), then try again. Otherwise, write directly to contact@strokeaction.org.
For members in charge of the contact@ inbox Check the inbox regularly and reply within 2 working days where possible. Requests about personal data (access, correction, deletion) must be handled within 30 days maximum (Privacy Policy, §8).

07 · The role of members

Keeping the website alive.

Every member is an ambassador for the website. Here is how to quickly point people in the right direction.

Someone tells you…Send them this link
"I want to become a member."strokeaction.org/en/nous-soutenir#devenir-membre
"I want to help / volunteer."strokeaction.org/en/nous-soutenir#benevolat
"How do I recognize a stroke?"strokeaction.org/en/education-avc
"Who runs the association?"strokeaction.org/en/gouvernance
"I'd like to read your bylaws."strokeaction.org/en/documents-cles
"How do I pay my membership fee?"strokeaction.org/en/faq
"I'm a doctor and want to collaborate."strokeaction.org/en/contact
"I'd like to make a donation."strokeaction.org/en/contact (until online payment is live)

For a French speaker, simply replace /en/ with /fr/. The page addresses keep their French names in both languages.

Share on social media

  • Follow and share our Facebook, LinkedIn and YouTube pages (links at the bottom of every page).
  • Spread the FAST method: everyone who knows it can save a life.
  • Rally people around World Stroke Day — 29 October.
  • Invite friends and family to sign up for the newsletter (News page).

Suggest content

The website grows thanks to you. Send the website team:

  • Photos of activities (for the Gallery) — with the consent of the people pictured.
  • Article ideas or event reports (News).
  • Testimonials from patients or families (with their written consent).
  • New official documents (strategic plan, annual report…).
Golden rules Never publish personal or medical data without consent · Always remind people that the website is informational and does not replace medical advice · Respect the brand guidelines (blue #2A9DF4 / red #F42A2A).
